## Part 6 — Surgical restoration of the inline DSL contract (mandated)

**Directive:** restore `embed! { name: X, script: { … } | "…", out_dir: "…" }` and
`prepare! { … }` executing inline in the proc-macro, on TOP of the hardened
engine. No nuclear revert: all fs/process/core hardening (GuardedPath sandbox,
`UnguardedPath::external`, lock-before-marker tempdirs, `ChildHandle::drop`
reaping, `Arc` env maps, `exec/` split) is retained untouched.

**Deliberate divergence from the verbatim restore:** the old
`.git`/`CARGO_PRIMARY_PACKAGE`/reuse-vs-rebuild gating is NOT restored verbatim —
it is REPLACED by a content-hash cache (review directive): the macro fingerprints
its inputs (script text + statically resolvable input files) and skips
re-execution when `<out_dir>/.oxdock_hash` matches. This removes the rebuild-
on-every-cargo-check hazard without env knobs or registry gates.
`OXDOCK_EMBED_FORCE_REBUILD` stays purged (the fingerprint supersedes it).
